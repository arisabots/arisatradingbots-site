2026-10-10 (last one, we promise)

Found and fixed a real safety-net bug we'd actually already written down and never circled back to

Real-money bots. Restarted and running - affects all three crypto bots, not just two

A real loss today (-9.2% on one position) led somewhere we didn't expect: a genuine bug in a shared safety mechanism, one we'd actually already noticed and written down three months ago without ever fixing.

Here's the mechanism, in plain terms. Our trailing stop - the thing that follows a position's best price and sells if it falls back too far - widens its own safety margin during choppier price action, which is normally sensible: don't get shaken out by ordinary noise. But the "how choppy is this" measurement it uses looks at very recent price swings. During a real crash, the crash itself IS the recent price action, so the measurement reads "very choppy" and widens the safety margin - precisely while a position is already in trouble. The faster it falls, the looser its own net gets. We'd actually flagged this exact risk in our own notes back in July and never came back to close it.

We tried the obvious fix first: just cap how wide that margin is allowed to get. Tested against real recorded data and it didn't hold up - the results bounced around unpredictably depending on the exact cap chosen, a sign of coincidence rather than a real effect, the same trap we caught ourselves falling into with a different bot earlier this week.

The fix that actually worked: freeze that "how choppy" measurement at whatever it read the last time the position hit a genuine new high, instead of letting it keep updating while a position is already sliding. That breaks the loop directly - a position already in trouble can no longer loosen its own safety net further. Tested against six days of real recorded data: a genuine net improvement, consistent in the more recent half of that window, essentially a wash (not a regression) in the older half.

One honest caveat: this specific fix wouldn't have fully saved today's exact loss - a single real price jump skipped past both the old and new safety level in one check, a separate, already-known limitation about how often we check prices, not about the math itself. Still a real, validated improvement to a mechanism all three of our crypto bots share. Ready and tested, not yet live - we restart these ourselves.
